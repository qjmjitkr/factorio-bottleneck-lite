## 📖 About

🔹 **Factorio — Bottleneck Lite** is a powerful tool built to help you identify production bottlenecks in your Factorio factory with color-coded machine status indicators. Green means running, yellow means waiting for input, red means output full — spot bottlenecks instantly at a glance.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **Factorio — Bottleneck Lite** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box. Join thousands of users who've already streamlined their Factorio modding workflow. Download now and get started in minutes. 🚀

## 🚀 Features

| **Feature** | **Description** |
|:---|:---|
| **Status Indicators** | Color-coded dots on every machine show its current operational state |
| **Low Resource Alert** | Yellow indicator when machines are starved of input materials |
| **Output Full Warning** | Red indicator when output belts or chests are full and blocking |
| **Performance Light** | Minimal UPS impact even in factories with thousands of machines |
